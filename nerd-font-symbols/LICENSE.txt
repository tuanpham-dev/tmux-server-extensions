Symbols Nerd Font extension — bundles the symbols-only face from the Nerd
Fonts project (Symbols Nerd Font Mono), under the MIT license. The upstream
license text follows.

Symbols Nerd Font Mono
  Nerd Fonts — https://github.com/ryanoasis/nerd-fonts
  The bundled woff2 is converted, without subsetting, from the
  SymbolsNerdFontMono-Regular.ttf that tmux-server itself shipped before
  commit 56e7c22 removed it; all 10,413 glyphs are preserved.

  Individual glyphs originate from the icon sets Nerd Fonts aggregates
  (Powerline, Font Awesome, Devicons, Octicons, Material Design Icons, Weather
  Icons, Seti-UI and others), each under its own upstream license as documented
  at https://github.com/ryanoasis/nerd-fonts#glyph-sets.
